1 The Pinhole Camera

  • A pinhole lets exactly one ray through per scene point
  • The image is inverted and scaled by f / Z
  • Far objects appear smaller than near ones
x = f · X / Z     y = f · Y / Z

2 Perspective Projection

  • Pixel coordinates follow from the intrinsics K
  • The extrinsics [R | t] map world points into the camera frame
  • λ equals the depth of the point in the camera frame
λ (u, v, 1)ᵀ = K [R | t] PW

3 Lens Distortion

  • Real lenses bend straight lines near the image border
  • Radial distortion grows with the distance r from the center
  • Barrel distortion: k₁ < 0, pincushion distortion: k₁ > 0
ud = (1 + k₁ r²)(u − u₀) + u₀

4 Calibration

  • Estimate K, R, t and the distortion from known 3D–2D pairs
  • DLT gives a linear solution from at least 6 points
  • Refine everything by minimizing the reprojection error
